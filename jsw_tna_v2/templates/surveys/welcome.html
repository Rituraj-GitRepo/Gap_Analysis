How This Portal Works

1
Enter Your Details
Provide your name, employee code and select your role (GET, MT, or Functional Head).
2
Select Your Function
Choose your function. You will see competencies specific to your area, derived directly from the JSW Motors Functional KPI Scorecard.
3
Complete the Survey
Functional Heads: prioritise competencies & set Day-1 levels. GETs/MTs: self-assess your current proficiency honestly.

📝 Functional Head Survey

  • Rate each competency by importance (1–5 scale)
  • Select your Top 10 critical competencies
  • Set minimum Day-1 readiness level per competency
  • Answer 3 open-ended strategy questions

👤 Employee Self-Assessment (GET / MT)

  • Rate your current proficiency level (1–5 scale)
  • Add evidence notes for key competencies
  • Reflect on strengths and development areas
  • Feeds directly into your Individual Development Plan
